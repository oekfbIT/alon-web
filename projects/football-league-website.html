Football League Website

Public site for leagues across Austria: fixtures, live scores, tables, leaderboards, team and player pages, AI previews, transfers, suspensions and search.

Try it live ↗About my role at ÖKFB

Overview

The public website and app of the Austrian Small-Field Football Federation, where fans, players and clubs follow around 200 clubs and up to 10,000 registered players across Austria's regional leagues.

Visitors pick a region and a league and land on everything around it: fixtures by season and matchday, league tables, live scores, team and player pages, player statistics, news, transfers, suspensions and disciplinary decisions, with search across players and teams.

The site has no separate editorial workflow for results. Referees record goals and cards in the referee app during the match, the backend saves every event, and scores and tables update live on the homepage and in the app. Before each matchday, AI-written match previews and reviews are generated from the league's own verified data.

Under the hood it is a React and TypeScript front end on top of the same Swift and Vapor backend that powers the admin dashboard, the team app and the referee app, so every part of the federation works from one source of truth.

What fans can follow

  1. Fixtures. Every match by season and matchday, with kick-off time, venue and a link to its preview.
  2. Live scores. Results update during the game, straight from the referee app.
  3. Tables and stats. League tables, top scorers and player statistics, calculated automatically.
  4. Teams and players. Squad pages, player profiles, transfers and suspensions.
  5. AI previews and reviews. Generated match previews and matchday reports in German, based on verified data.
