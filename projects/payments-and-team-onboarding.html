Payments & Team Onboarding

Document-based team registration and Stripe payments with verified webhooks, idempotent credits, versioned fees, invoices and recovery after interruptions.

Read the technical deep diveAbout my role at ÖKFB

Overview

Registering a new team used to mean the federation office processing every application by hand: emails, paper contracts, payment checks and data entry. Now the whole path from application to a live team account runs automatically, and an admin only verifies the submitted information.

That saves more than two weeks of work per registration. Payments run through Stripe, and every step is designed for retries, interruptions and duplicates, so money and data stay correct.

From application to live team

  1. Apply. The team applies and receives a welcome email with contract downloads and a protected upload link.
  2. Verify email. A signed, expiring email link confirms the applicant’s address.
  3. Upload documents. ID documents and signed contracts are uploaded. Partial submissions are allowed, but the set must be complete before the next step.
  4. Review. An admin verifies the information instead of processing it by hand.
  5. Pay. The fee is paid through Stripe. The balance is credited only after Stripe confirms the payment.
  6. Go live. The team is assigned to a league and created. The invoice and confirmation email are generated automatically.

What I built

  • Document-based registration workflow with a protected upload link and required-document checks.
  • Stripe Checkout and PaymentIntents with signed webhooks, so only verified payments are credited.
  • Idempotency keys and durable receipts against double credits and debits.
  • Versioned fees: every financial action stores the fee version it used.
  • Automatic invoices and confirmation emails, with an invoice history per team.
  • Background jobs on Vapor Queues that resume interrupted payment, accounting and email processing.
