Team Self-Service Portal

Teams manage their squad, register players and trainers, upload photos, follow contracts and finances, request transfers and message the office.

Try it live ↗About my role at ÖKFB

Overview

The team app is each club's own office. Team managers handle their squad, matches and finances themselves instead of emailing the federation.

Lineups are submitted ahead of each match, so only authorised players can go onto the pitch.

What teams do in the app

  1. Lineup. Teams build their own lineup from their available players and submit it ahead of time.
  2. Match day. They see the kits, venue and weather conditions, and can postpone or cancel a game within the league limits.
  3. Players and staff. They register new players, update player details and photos, and upload trainer information.
  4. Office. They follow their balance and invoices, chat with the federation and look up the league rules.
