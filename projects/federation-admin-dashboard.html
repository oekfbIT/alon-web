Federation Admin Dashboard

Leagues, seasons, fixtures, referees, team applications, discipline, transfers, fees, content, email templates, push notifications and analytics.

Read the technical deep diveAbout my role at ÖKFB

Overview

The control centre for the whole league. Federation staff run every part of league operations from one place, with a global search across players, teams, referees and leagues.

What used to be spreadsheets, emails and paper forms is now a set of screens that feed the team app, the referee app and the public website.

What admins control

  1. Leagues and fixtures. Create leagues, seasons and matchdays, manage stadiums and handle reschedule requests.
  2. Referees. Manage referees and assign them to games.
  3. Teams and players. Manage teams and logins, approve player eligibility and run transfers.
  4. Registrations and discipline. Review new team registrations and manage disciplinary cases.
  5. Finance and messages. Set fees, create invoices and answer teams in the shared inbox.
  6. Content and notifications. Edit homepage content, email templates and push notifications.
