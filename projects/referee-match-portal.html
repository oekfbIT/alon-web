Referee Match Portal

Referees run matches from their phone: match sheets, kick-off to full time, goals and cards, corrections, no-shows and the match report.

Read the technical deep diveAbout my role at ÖKFB

Overview

The referee app replaces the paper match sheet. Administrators assign referees to games in the admin dashboard, and the referee runs the match on the spot from their phone.

Everything recorded during the game goes straight to the backend, so nobody writes results down and types them in again later. The same data drives the live score on the federation homepage.

How it works

  1. Assign. The administrator assigns referees to specific games.
  2. Notify. The referee gets a notification for the match.
  3. Run the match. On the pitch, the referee starts the game, runs the halves and records goals and yellow, red and yellow-red cards.
  4. Save. Match information and events are saved to the backend as they happen.
  5. Live score. Fans follow the score live on the homepage.
