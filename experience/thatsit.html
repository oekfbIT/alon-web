The product

Buy, sell and swap trading and sports cards in one place: Pokémon, One Piece, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, sports cards and more, graded or raw, with protected payments. Card-for-card swaps carry no commission.

The app

iOS and Android app · launching mid-December 2026
  • Scan to identifyPoint the camera at a card and the app recognises it and shows its value, one card after another.
  • One cart, one paymentBuy from several vendors at once: one payment at checkout, separate orders per vendor.
  • Logistics handledA built-in logistics service takes the headache out of shipping: delivery options, carrier coverage and tracking are worked out at checkout, so sellers only have to drop the parcel off.

What I did

  • Built AI card matching in Python with FastAPI, PyTorch and Transformers: DINOv2 image embeddings and Qdrant vector search link each listing to the right card.
  • Run it as containerised services next to a React Native app and a Swift backend.
  • Own product vision, roadmap and launch plan; lead sprint planning and retrospectives.
  • Keep the budget in check, including the running costs of AI tools, cloud services and infrastructure.

What I learned

The interesting problems in applied AI are often not about the model. They are about data, interfaces, cost and how the rest of the system behaves when the model isn’t sure.
