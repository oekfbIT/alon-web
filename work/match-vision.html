Overview

Systems like Veo put AI cameras at the pitch. I’m building the analysis side as software: take an existing recording or a live stream from any camera, and turn it into match data automatically.

The goal: detect shots and other key events, track where players move on the pitch, and identify exactly who each player is when the squad has been preloaded, to make our league feel professional without professional budgets.

It builds directly on the federation platform, which already knows every team, squad and fixture. The preview at the top of this page shows where it’s heading.

Training data

Detection starts with good labels. I annotate match footage in Label Studio with six classes, then train YOLOv8 on our own pitches, kits and camera angles instead of relying on generic sports datasets.

Target pipeline, from video to match data.

DetectionYOLOv8, trained on my Label Studio annotations: players, goalkeepers, referees, ball and goals
BackendDetections flow into my backend, where they can be joined with fixtures, teams and squads
MCP + vision modelAn MCP server gives a vision model on the stream access to tools and match context, so it can reason about what is happening, not just what is visible
EventsShots and other key moments, turned into timestamps and clips
PositionsWhere players move, mapped from the camera view onto the pitch
IdentityWho each player is, using preloaded squad data such as jersey numbers

Detection, labelling, backend and MCP are in place; events, positions and identity are what I’m building now.

How I’m approaching it

Use the data we already have

The federation already stores every squad, jersey number and fixture. Identity becomes a matching problem against known players, not open-ended face recognition.

Specialised models first, a general model on top

YOLOv8 does the fast, frame-level work. The vision model, reached through MCP, handles context and judgement, the same split I use for AI match previews: compute what you can, let the model decide only what it must.

Works with any camera

Recordings and streams from ordinary cameras, so clubs don’t need dedicated hardware.
