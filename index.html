    Avi Ben-Or

    President, Austrian Small-Field Football Federation (ÖKFB) · Interim reference

    The Austrian Small-Field Football Federation (ÖKFB) has existed for over 25 years and is dedicated to organising, promoting and developing small-field football in Austria. With around 200 clubs and up to 10,000 registered players, the federation runs an extensive league and match operation.

    Mr. Alon Yakobichvili has been a volunteer board member of the ÖKFB since November 2023. As Head of Technology, he is responsible for the technology strategy, digitalisation and further development of the federation's entire digital infrastructure.

    As part of a comprehensive technological modernisation, he independently took on the design, architecture and development of a centralised digital platform. Today it is the technical foundation for nearly all organisational and sporting processes of the federation and connects all relevant user groups: players, team managers, referees, administrators, club officials and fans.

    His main tasks and responsibilities include:

    • Independent design, architecture and development of the central IT infrastructure, including back-end systems, databases, interfaces and automated business processes
    • Overall technical responsibility for five interconnected digital platforms: web, mobile, referee administration, live scoring and administration
    • Development and introduction of automated processes for match organisation, team administration, result recording, notifications and administrative tasks
    • Building a live-scoring infrastructure that processes and publishes results for around 150 matches on regular Sundays
    • Independent management of the technology product strategy, roadmap, project prioritisation, and coordination of external development partners and releases
    • Introduction of agile working methods based on Scrum and Kanban, including structured planning, development, documentation and handover processes
    • Sustainable organisational structures that keep the platforms developing and running reliably, independent of his personal presence
    • Strategic advice to the board on digitalisation, technology investments, efficiency and long-term scalability

    Through the solutions he independently developed and implemented, more than 80% of the previously manual administrative routine tasks could be automated. This brought considerable relief to operational staff and a lasting increase in organisational efficiency.

    Particularly noteworthy is the federation-wide reach of his work. The system landscape he created supports all organisational levels and user groups and enables the largely automated running of a complex league operation with thousands of participants.

    Mr. Yakobichvili impresses with his exceptional technical expertise, his pronounced entrepreneurial thinking and his outstanding ability to independently turn complex requirements into scalable and economically sustainable solutions. He shows an exceptionally high degree of initiative, sense of responsibility and strategic foresight.

    In his board and leadership role, he demonstrates excellent organisational and decision-making competence. He takes responsibility for technical projects with the greatest possible independence, sets clear priorities, and ensures lasting project organisation through structured processes, targeted delegation and transparent communication.

    He carries out this work on a volunteer basis and alongside his professional commitments. His exceptional commitment and high reliability contribute significantly to the continuous technological and organisational development of the federation.

    Mr. Yakobichvili always fulfils the tasks he has taken on to our fullest satisfaction and in every respect. His performance enjoys the highest recognition within the board, and his conduct towards board members, employees, clubs and external partners is exemplary at all times.

    This interim reference is issued at Mr. Yakobichvili's request. He continues to serve as a volunteer board member and Head of Technology. We thank him for his consistently excellent performance to date and look forward to continued successful cooperation.

    Avi Ben-Or, President and Chairman (Obmann) of the ÖKFB
